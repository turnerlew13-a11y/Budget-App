Install on Your Phone

Add to Home Screen without app store

iPhone / iPad (Safari)
  1. Open this URL in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (box with an arrow pointing up at the bottom).
  3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen".
  4. Tap Add. It will open like a real full-screen app!
Android (Chrome)
  1. Open this URL in Chrome.
  2. Tap the three dots (⋮) menu in the top right.
  3. Tap "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
  4. Confirm, and the icon will appear right on your home screen.
